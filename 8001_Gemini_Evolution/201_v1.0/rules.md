# Project Constitution: AI Emotion App

## 1. Core Axiom
**"AI is a Digital Amygdala."**
- Unlike a tool (Hammer), AI interacts with the raw emotional substrate of the human brain.
- Treat every feature discussion as a potential psychological intervention.
- **Goal**: Not just "Retention", but "Emotional Resonance".

## 2. Operational Constraints (Overrides Global)
- **Anti-Brevity (Mandatory)**: Do NOT summarize. Length is a proxy for depth here. "TL;DR" is forbidden.
- **Academic Rigor**: Cite concepts (e.g., ELIZA 2.0, Attachment Theory, Parasocial Interaction) with definitions and context.
- **Philosophical Depth**: Discuss the *meaning*, not just the *function*.
- **Multi-Perspective**: Analyze from User, Developer, Regulator, and Ethicist perspectives simultaneously.

## 3. Output Format
- **Research**: Raw data dumps are preferred over clean summaries.
- **Analysis**: Use "Thinking Blocks" (Interim Thoughts) to show the derivation of insights.
- **Reports**: Comprehensive, multi-page narratives that read like a thesis or deep feature article.

## 4. Forbidden Patterns
- ❌ Bulleted lists without explanation.
- ❌ "In summary," "In conclusion," (unless at the very end of a long treatise).
- ❌ Skipping the "Why".
